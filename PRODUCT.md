# SecFlow
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Django, SQLite, server-rendered HTML, CSS and JavaScript, confirmed from BLUEPRINT.md.
## Users and purpose
University prototype for analysts investigating simulated instant payments. Admin manages imports, users and algorithm settings; analysts investigate and correct restrictions.
## Capabilities and constraints
Five primary pages, auditable decisions, explainable risk, and from-scratch Isolation Forest, DBSCAN and LOF. PaySim and generated scenarios are separate evaluation populations. No banking integration. Scores are not calibrated probabilities.
## Brand commitments
English interface. Minimalist blue and white. Direct code build approved by the user.
## Evidence
BLUEPRINT.md and data/generated/manifest.json are the current specification and dataset evidence. Older non-ML plans are superseded.
